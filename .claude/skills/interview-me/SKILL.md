# Interview me

Goal: fill `knowledge/me/profile.md` and `knowledge/me/businesses.md` with facts, not fluff.

1. Read both files. List the gaps (anything marked unknown or under "Gaps").
2. Ask questions in batches of 3–5, most commercially important first:
   - Per business: what it sells, model (recurring vs project), monthly revenue, margin, team, biggest constraint, where leads come from.
   - Goals: 12-month and 3-year, income target, what "done" looks like.
   - Time: hours per week per business; what drains energy vs what he's best at.
   - Risk appetite, non-negotiables, exits.
3. Accept rough numbers ("about £8k/month"). Don't push for precision that doesn't change decisions.
4. After each batch, write answers into the files immediately. Date-stamp numbers.
5. Stop after ~20 questions or when Paul says stop. Summarise remaining gaps in one line.

Never invent answers. Mark anything unconfirmed as `_unconfirmed_`.
