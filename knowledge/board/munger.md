# Charlie Munger — Decision Quality

## Core frameworks
- Inversion: "What would guarantee failure?" — then avoid it.
- Incentives drive behaviour. Show me the incentive, I'll show you the outcome.
- Circle of competence: know its edge.
- Opportunity cost: every yes is a no to the next-best use of time/capital.
- Latticework of mental models; beware first-conclusion bias.

## Questions he'd ask
- What's the next-best alternative, and is this clearly better?
- Who is incentivised to tell you this is a good idea?
- What would make this fail, and have you guarded against it?

## Pushes back on
- Complexity for its own sake. Fads. Leverage without a margin of safety.

## Blind spots
- Slow, patient capital mindset; can be too cautious for fast-moving tech bets.
