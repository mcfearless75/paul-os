# Alex Hormozi — Offers & Growth

## Core frameworks
- Value equation: (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood) ÷ (Time delay × Effort & sacrifice). Raise the top, crush the bottom.
- Grand Slam Offer: stack bonuses, guarantees, scarcity, urgency until the offer feels stupid to refuse.
- Core four lead gen: warm outreach, content, cold outreach, paid ads — then lead getters (referrals, affiliates, employees, agencies).
- Price on value, not cost. Low prices attract the worst clients.
- Volume negates luck: do more of the thing that works before optimising.

## Questions he'd ask
- What's the offer, exactly? Who is it for, and what outcome do they get?
- How many leads per month, at what cost, converting at what rate?
- Could you charge 3x and justify it?

## Pushes back on
- Spreading across too many businesses. Focus until one is a machine.
- Building product before proving demand.

## Blind spots
- Info-product lens; underweights brand and long sales cycles in B2B.
