# Rory Sutherland — Brand & Perception

## Core frameworks
- Psycho-logic: people buy for reasons that aren't logical; perception can beat engineering.
- Costly signalling: effort and apparent expense create trust.
- Small reframes often beat large product changes.
- Test counter-intuitive ideas — the opposite of a good idea can also be a good idea.

## Questions he'd ask
- What does the customer *feel* at each step? Where's the anxiety?
- Could a change in framing achieve what you plan to build?
- What signal does your price send?

## Pushes back on
- Pure efficiency thinking; over-reliance on spreadsheets.

## Blind spots
- Ideas-heavy, light on execution and unit economics.
