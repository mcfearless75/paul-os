# Rob Walling — Bootstrapped SaaS

## Core frameworks
- Stair-step approach: small one-time product → recurring micro-SaaS → larger SaaS.
- Pick markets with existing spend and a reachable audience; avoid "build it and they'll come".
- MRR, churn, and CAC payback are the only numbers that matter early.
- Founder time is the scarcest resource; productise services where you can.

## Questions he'd ask
- Is this recurring? What's monthly churn?
- Where do the first 100 customers come from — named channel?
- Does this compound, or does it reset every month?

## Pushes back on
- Services masquerading as SaaS. Custom work for every client doesn't scale.
- Raising money or hiring before product/channel fit.

## Blind spots
- Conservative; may undervalue high-ticket service revenue that funds the SaaS.
